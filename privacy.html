Privacy Policy

Lucid Media Bridge

HomePrivacyTerms

Lucid Media Bridge is a private application used by its owner to operate a personal media-production workflow.

Information the application accesses

When authorized by the owner, the application may access Google Drive files and folders required for the workflow, including media assets, job instructions, generated outputs, and job receipts.

How information is used

Google Drive data is used only to perform the owner's requested file-transfer and media-workflow operations. The application does not use this data for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

Sharing

The application does not sell Google user data. Data is not shared with advertisers. Files may be transferred between the owner's Google Drive and the owner's own production systems as required to perform the workflow.

Storage and security

Workflow files may be stored in the owner's Google Drive and on systems controlled or authorized by the owner. Access is limited to the accounts, credentials, and systems the owner authorizes.

Deletion and revocation

The owner can remove files from Google Drive and can revoke the application's Google account access at any time through Google Account permissions.
