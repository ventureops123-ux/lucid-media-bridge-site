Lucid Media Bridge

Private media workflow for the owner's own Google Drive and production systems.

HomePrivacyTerms

What it does

Lucid Media Bridge is a private tool used by its owner to move media files, job instructions, and job receipts between the owner's Google Drive and the owner's production server.

The application is not offered as a public consumer service and does not sell access to third parties.

Google Drive access

Google Drive access is used only to read and write files that the owner explicitly authorizes for this workflow. The application does not use Google Drive data for advertising or sell it to third parties.
